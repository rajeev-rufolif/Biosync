What's actually going on under the hood

BioSync isn't a generic planner template. Every block in your week is placed for a reason — here's what it reasons about.

Circadian-rhythm scheduling

Your hardest subjects get placed in your real high-energy windows, not a generic 9-to-5 template. BioSync accounts for the natural early-afternoon alertness dip, your stated chronotype (early bird, night owl, or in between), and keeps demanding blocks away from your lowest-energy hours.

Reads your real syllabus

Upload your actual timetable or subject list — PDF, Word, Excel, or a photo. BioSync extracts your real subjects, weekly hours, and deadlines instead of asking you to type them in by hand.

Food & energy aware

Heavy or high-carb meals before a focus block cause a predictable energy dip — BioSync puts lighter food before deep work, and times your coffee or tea for when it'll actually help, not right before bed.

Deadline-aware prioritization

Exams and deadlines extracted from your documents automatically get extra focus blocks in the days leading up to them — so your schedule shifts as the week you actually have gets closer.

Realistic, not overloaded

BioSync respects the study hours per day you say are actually realistic for you — it won't pack your week with more deep-focus time than you told it you can handle.

Streaks to keep you consistent

Following your plan builds a streak, Duolingo-style — a small nudge to actually stick with the schedule, not just look at it once.

Transparent reasoning

Every schedule comes with a plain-language explanation of why it's shaped the way it is — not a black box. You can see exactly which of your answers shaped which part of your week.

Which AI runs this, and what to expect

Default · Groq free tier

Chat, document reading, and schedule building all run on Groq's free tier. It's fast, but free-tier access is rate-limited and can be unreliable when it's busy. If that happens, BioSync says so on the schedule page instead of showing an empty week, and you can retry a minute later.

Alternate · Claude (Anthropic)

Claude is supported as an alternate provider for deployments that configure an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, or per-session via the chat page's "Use my own AI API key" control. Unless switched over, you're on Groq.

Alternate · Gemini (Google AI)

Gemini is also supported as an alternate provider — same deal, via GEMINI_API_KEY or the chat page's own key option. Its free tier allows far more tokens per minute than Groq's, which is why it's worth trying if a schedule keeps failing to generate. Note: a consumer Gemini app subscription is not an API key and can't be used here — you'd need a free key from Google AI Studio.
