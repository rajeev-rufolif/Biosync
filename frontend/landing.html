What it is
An AI planner that schedules study around energy, not just free time.
Who it's for
Students preparing for placements and internships while keeping up with classes.
The problem
Placement prep and coursework fight for the same hours, and generic planners ignore when you actually focus.
What we built
Chat, document reading and schedule generation on a Flask and LLM pipeline.

From a chat to a week in three steps

  1. 1

    Chat about your routine

    Sleep, meals, caffeine, when you focus best and how many hours you can really study.

  2. 2

    Drop in your syllabus

    Upload a timetable or subject list. BioSync pulls out subjects, weekly hours and exam dates.

  3. 3

    Get a week with reasons

    Focus blocks, breaks and meals for each day, each with a note on why it sits there.

Every block is placed for a reason

FAQ

Does BioSync store my data?

Sessions live in server memory with no database, so a restart clears them. A key you paste is kept in your browser tab only and sent with your own requests.

How accurate is document reading?

Text PDFs, Word and Excel files read reliably. Photos use OCR, which can miss messy handwriting. A scanned PDF with no text layer may come back empty.

Why would a schedule fail to generate?

The default Groq free tier has a token-per-minute limit. You get a clear message and a retry, or you can switch to your own Claude or Gemini key.

Is this medical advice?

No. It uses general patterns like chronotype and the post-lunch dip from your answers. It does not measure your sleep or health.
